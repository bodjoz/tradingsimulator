    How you compare

    Every alternative invests your full starting capital on day 1 and holds to the end. Gross = no commission, spread or tax (for you: your same trades filled at the mid price). Net = whole shares with your broker's spread and commission, and your tax regime applied to the final sale. Indexes are bought as if through a fund priced at 100, over the same calendar dates as your charts; with charts from different periods, capital is split equally across those periods. Index levels exclude dividends (except the DAX), so they understate what a real index fund returned. Annual rates are compounded over game years (252 trading days) and are extrapolated for sessions under a year.
